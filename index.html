PRINCIPLES AND PROCESSES

Things I Believe In

Power of Knowledge

Good questions lead to good insights. I believe in deep research and asking "why" until we get to the root of the problem. Surface-level understanding leads to surface-level solutions.

Good Design is a State of Mind

Usability + Aesthetics = Emotions. Design isn't just about making things look pretty or work well. It's about creating experiences that make people feel confident, productive, and in control.

Collaboration is Key

Great experiences are hard to achieve alone. The best solutions emerge when designers, developers, product managers, and users work together. I design with, not for.

Change Pushes Innovation

Fail often and fail early in order to succeed. I embrace experimentation and iteration. The fear of being wrong shouldn't stop us from finding what's right.

ABOUT

Background

I'm a Senior Product Designer with 18+ years of experience designing enterprise B2B and SaaS products. Most recently, I spent 4 years at Bitdefender designing cybersecurity solutions for MSPs and co-creating the company-wide design system.

My approach combines strategic thinking with hands-on execution. I excel at breaking down complex technical requirements into intuitive interfaces that balance user needs with business constraints. I've worked across cybersecurity, healthcare, and business intelligence, always focusing on enterprise products where the stakes are high and the users are demanding.
