GravityZone Simplified

Role: Lead UX Designer | Timeline: 2023 | Location: Bitdefender, Cluj-Napoca

Simplifying complex enterprise security management for SMBs. A streamlined platform that reduced onboarding from hours to minutes while maintaining the power of GravityZone.

OVERVIEW

The Challenge

Something that was missing but always on the minds of our users: a simpler approach to using the big and complex GravityZone platform.

We were working with multiple platforms serving different users - from SMBs to enterprise players - and everyone had different needs. The existing platform was powerful but overwhelming for smaller businesses who made up 70% of our user base.

  • Complex onboarding process taking hours to complete
  • Overwhelming interface for SMB users with 70% of user base
  • Multiple platforms with inconsistent experiences
  • Need to balance simplicity with enterprise power users' requirements
TEAM & APPROACH

Collaborative Process

The team was set: Engineering Director, Product Manager, and myself as the UX Designer. We had over 12 hours of debates across different meetings, discussing trade-offs and iterations on what we would include in the MVP.

Feedback from end-users was crucial at this stage. Working across platforms with different use cases, from SMB to enterprise players, we needed to understand everyone's specific needs before making decisions.

My Role

  • Led the UX design strategy and user research
  • Designed user flows and wireframes for the simplified experience
  • Facilitated weekly meetings with Engineering and Product
  • Worked closely with Engineering to maximize existing code and design assets
  • Created the template system for 2-3 click onboarding
SOLUTION

A Dual-Mode Platform

The main goal evolved into creating a new platform, connected to the full GravityZone console but accessed differently. We focused on SMBs as they represented 70% of our users, making their needs most crucial.

The solution: an easier, faster, and more upfront way of onboarding new clients and managing them.

Simplified Onboarding

Reduced complex company setup to just name, industry type, and a few license options. Preset policies made security configuration instant instead of overwhelming.

Template System

Users could save any configuration as a template. Second company onboarding? Just select your template and complete in 2-3 clicks.

Code Reuse Strategy

Worked closely with Engineering to maximize existing code and design components, speeding up development while maintaining consistency.

Advanced Mode Toggle

For tech-savvy users and admins, maintained access to the full GravityZone platform by simply switching between "Simplified" and "Advanced" modes.

KEY LEARNINGS

What I Learned

Simplification doesn't mean removing features

The biggest challenge was resisting the urge to strip away powerful features. Instead, we learned to organize complexity through progressive disclosure and smart defaults.

By creating a template system and preset configurations, we made the platform feel simple for new users while keeping all the power accessible when needed. The "Simplified" vs "Advanced" mode toggle was crucial - it gave users confidence that nothing was lost, just reorganized.

Collaboration with Engineering

Working closely with the Engineering Director from day one was essential. By understanding technical constraints early, we could design solutions that maximized existing code and components, significantly reducing development time while maintaining quality.

User Segmentation

Not all users need the same thing. While 70% of users (SMBs) needed simplicity, the remaining 30% needed power. Building for both through a mode toggle, rather than creating separate products, kept the platform unified while serving different needs.
